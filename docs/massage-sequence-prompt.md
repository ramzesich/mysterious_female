1. Remove the masseuse, the empty table with towels, the table with the guest, and the round table.

3. Generate a few candidates for massage tables. The tables should look ready for a person to lie down and have a massage.
4. When a candidate is chosen, place two of such tables at their current places.
5. Generate a normally-sized masseuse with a sequence of animations:
    a) Standing, in profile, stretching.
    b) Giving a massage with their back to us: bowing slightly, giving a massage with their hands, straightening up.
    c) Same animation, but facing us.
    d) Other animations, needed to create the massage sequence: walking around the massage table and giving massage when, with their back to us, obscuring the table with the client, and, facing us, standing behind the table and the client. When standing at the head or at the feet: the stretching animation.
6. If we press E when we're at the massage table with the client, the masseuse goes to the farther end of the massage table, relatively to us, and while they are stretching, the client comes up and leaves off the screen, opening any doors on the way, if needed. Then, the client is removed.
7. While the client is leaving, we are lying down on the table, having a few rounds of the massage, standing up and being given our massage gun.
8. Any next visit to the scene has the client there on the table again.
9. We can have our massage any time we want, in the same way, just without obtaining anything, if we already got our massage gun.
