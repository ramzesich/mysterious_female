# Welcome screen

"Bumbusik" in the center of the screen.
In the dimmed and out-of-focus background:
The sun shining in the center of the screen, surrounded by a few clouds.

Below, a two big buttons, one below the other:
PLAY
LANGUAGE

PLAY triggers the sequense described in the **Start sequence** below.
LANGUAGE lets player swtich between English (default choice) and Ukrainian.

## Start sequence

Start menu dissipates and dimmed background comes into focus while gaining the gameplay level of brightness.
It smoothly transitions into **Game intro** described below.

# Game intro

The scene starts with the sun shining in the center of the screen, surrounded by a few clouds.
Camera moves down and shows the sea and the Baltic shoreline in the background. Birds are flying, sunbeams shimmer on the sea waves.
Camera moves right and the ship comes into view from the right. When the ship is fully shown, camera zooms in to deck 3, revealing its small corridor part.
The camera continues to zoom in at a door with the sign "Crew" above it until her crew cabin is revealed with Bumbusik standing by the mirror.
The zoom continues at Bumbusik, showing a zoomed in and more detailed character. She adjusts her name badge and turns away from the mirror, facing the cabin door.
Camera zooms out to the usual gameplay scale, seamlessly replacing Bumbusik with her usual gameplay character.
The controls are given to the player, the game HUD shows, and the game starts.
