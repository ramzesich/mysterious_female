# Welcome screen

"Bumbusik" in the center of the screen, in pixel capitals (Press Start 2P) coloured like the ship's sun livery.
In the dimmed and out-of-focus background:
The sun shining in the center of the screen, soft and blooming, surrounded by a few soft clouds, with sunbeams falling through the gaps between them.

Below, two big buttons, one below the other, as brass plates with cream lettering (gold for the chosen one):
PLAY
LANGUAGE

The whole menu stands a little above the middle of the screen.
It is chosen with ↑/↓ and Enter, Space or E, or with the mouse.

PLAY triggers the sequence described in the **Start sequence** below.
LANGUAGE lets the player switch between English (default choice) and Ukrainian. The menu is in capitals in both languages.

## Start sequence

The start menu dissipates and the dimmed background comes into focus while gaining the gameplay level of brightness.
It smoothly transitions into the **Game intro** described below.

# Game intro

The scene starts with the sun shining in the center of the screen, surrounded by a few clouds, its sunbeams falling through them.
The camera moves down and shows the sea and the Baltic shoreline in the background. Gulls are flying; under the sun a path of light glitters on the sea, glint by glint. Nothing in the distance drifts: the camera stands still.
The camera moves right and the ship comes into view from the right. When the ship is fully shown, the camera zooms in on deck 3. Only near the end of the zoom does deck 3's side open, revealing its small crew corridor.
The camera stops there at the usual gameplay scale, on the door with the sign "CREW" over it: her crew cabin's door.
The door opens and Bumbusik walks out of it, a few steps to the left (a walk, not a run), and stops. She straightens her name badge, then turns to go on to the left.
The controls are given to the player and the game starts. The HUD is empty at first: each count shows up with its first find.
A popup over her says how to move and jump ("← and → to move, Space to jump", or the controller's buttons when she plays with a controller), for three seconds.
The first time she walks past her door, a popup says "E to interact".

A game already under way gets the same welcome screen and the same sky, sea and ship, a little quicker; then the camera closes in on wherever she is and fades over to the game.
Esc ends the intro at once.
