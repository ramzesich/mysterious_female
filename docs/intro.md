# Game intro

The scene starts with the sea and the background shown on screen. Birds are flying, sunbeams shimmer on the sea waves.
Camera moves right and the ship comes into view from the right. When the ship is fully shown, camera zooms in to deck 3's corridor.
A door with the sign "Crew" above it opens, and Bumbusik steps out of it, adjusting her name badge.
The controls are given to the player, and the game starts.
