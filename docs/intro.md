# Game intro

The scene starts with the sun shining in the center of the screen, surrounded by a few clouds.
Camera moves down and shows the sea and the Baltic shoreline in the background. Birds are flying, sunbeams shimmer on the sea waves.
Camera moves right and the ship comes into view from the right. When the ship is fully shown, camera zooms in to deck 3, revealing its small corridor part.
The camera continues to zoom in at a door with the sign "Crew" above it. The door is zoomed in and appears centered and much larger than when during the gameplay.
The door opens, and Bumbusik (a zoomed in and more detailed character) steps out of it, adjusting her name badge.
Camera zooms out to the usual gameplay scale, seamlessly replacing Bumbusik with her usual gameplay character.
The controls are given to the player, the game HUD shows, and the game starts.
