# The game's progression, start to finale

Only the way through: what she must do, what it opens, and what she learns on the way. Books, things to find,
tangerines, secrets, quirks, deliveries, look-outs and the optional outside (region 7) are left out. One optional
area is named because a step of the story opens it: deck 10's midships hallway, under the drained pool.

Drawn from `GAME_DESIGN.md` §6.1, `docs/population.md` (the later word where they differ) and what is built
(2026-11-23). The maps: `docs/ship-regions.png` (the ship's ten regions) and `docs/ship-unlock-order.png` (the
order, step by step). Some captions in those maps are older than the build: step 2's "the forward stairs' fire
door" (it is the midships crew trunk), step 1's "the dash" (it is the lunge), step 4's "Bumbot in the towels"
(he is on the bottle shelf now), and the linen room's door, drawn at 112 m (it is one cabin door aft, at 115.4 m,
since 2026-11-23).

## The story in one line

A stowaway cat, Bumbot, is loose on the ship. Bumbusik, a crew member, does her colleagues' errands. Each errand
earns her a department's key card, and each card opens the next part of the ship. Bumbot always escapes at the
last moment, and his escapes open new ways too. At sunset he waits for her at the tip of the bow.

## The story

**A morning below decks.** The day starts quietly on deck 3, in the crew corridor, where Bumbusik steps out of her
cabin, straightens her badge and climbs the service ladder to work. Word has gone round that a stowaway cat has been
spotted aboard, and the ship's security officer has roped off the guest stairs with his net in hand, peering up them
with great seriousness. None of that is her concern yet. At the gangway an officer is stuck at his post with an empty
stomach, so she goes to fetch his lunch from the Seven Seas.

There, on the counter, a black cat has his face in the officer's plate.

It is love at first sight. "Кісюнічка!!!" she cries, and lunges, and the cat is gone along the counter in a shower of
tangerines. From that moment the cat is hers to catch, and the cry is her battle cry. She carries a fresh tray to
the gangway without spilling it, and the grateful officer hands her his Deck card: she is doing his rounds now. The
card opens the crew trunk, and up she goes.

**Among the guests.** Deck 6 is a different world: the atrium's glass and brass, the shops, the promenade, the sea
beyond the windows. She catches sight of Bumbot riding the glass elevator up to decks she can't reach yet, as if he
were showing her the way. She finds him again in the Library, curled up asleep on top of the book return trolley.
She creeps up on tiptoe, he wakes, and he bolts through the roped-off door at the back, knocking the "QUIET PLEASE" stand over
so that the door stays ajar behind him.

**The show must go on.** Behind that door is the theatre, mid-rehearsal, with the dancers on stage and the cat
sitting in the spotlight washing himself as though the show were his. She creeps up; the security officer, who has had
the same idea, charges out of the wings, leaps at the cat with his net and catches the stage's hanging lamp instead:
down he comes with it, through the stage's trap door, and up goes Bumbot on the fly rope at the other end of its line,
into the dark above the stage. Left behind is a stage manager sneezing helplessly, allergic to cats, whose pills are down
in his cabin on deck 4. She runs down for them, and he thanks her with the Entertainment card. It opens the crew way
up from Bliss, into housekeeping country.

**Housekeeping.** Deck 9's corridor is a working corridor: towels, vacuums, doors that fly open and guests who would
rather not be disturbed. At its far end a steward has his trolley wedged across the corridor. She puts her shoulder to
it and pushes it free (a new trick), and he gives her his Housekeeping card. The card opens the linen room, and there,
asleep on a shelf among the bottles, is Bumbot. A laundry cart stands between them. She pushes it toward him as quietly
as she can, and just as it is about to touch him he springs at the cover on the mouth of a ventilation shaft, sends it
clanging to the floor and slips into the dark opening. She pushes the cart on up to the mouth, climbs onto it and
crawls in after him.

**Home, unexpectedly.** The shaft tips her down a slope into the space between two decks and runs forward there, over
the lift halls and the atrium, the ship showing below through its grilles. The last grille gives way under her and
drops her into a calm corridor on deck 8, and among its doors is cabin 8206: her own cabin from the real cruise, the surprise she was never told about. It is her place to
come back to from now on. At the end of the corridor she sees the tip of a black tail vanish up the forward stairs.

**The Lido.** The stairs lead all the way up to deck 11, into the sun. In the gym she finds a pair of grip socks, and
suddenly walls are something to climb. By the pool, Bumbot is fishing at the water's edge. He slips away from her
once more, but a boy with a water pistol is less forgiving: one squirt and the cat is in the pool, scrambling out and
over the drain's lever, which goes over under him, and the pool empties behind him. In the bare tank lies a maintenance
hatch, a way she doesn't need
but may take: it drops her into deck 10's midships hallway, a corridor of guest cabins closed off until now, where a pot
of tea is waiting to be delivered. Once she has walked out through its lobby doors they stay open behind her. Aft, at the Great Outdoors, her husband sits at his table by the
ice cream machine, looking at the wake. Right beside him is her way up: a climb off the service station's roof and a
wall jump onto the sun cover, up to the top deck.

**The horn.** Up on deck 12 an engineer is waiting for a coffee he can't leave his post to get. She brings him one,
still hot, past the Teppanyaki chef's flames, and in return he takes her up the funnel, to the very top of the ship.
Bumbot is sitting up there on the funnel's frame, and the engineer lets her pull the horn's cord. The blast shakes the
ship: the cat shoots down the funnel and races toward the bow; the security officer leaps out of his skin, drops his
master card and runs for the bridge; the old lady on the sun deck flees her sunbed and leaves her parasol behind; the
gulls rise off the bow in a cloud. Bumbusik picks up the card and the parasol.

**A wedding in the way.** At the front of the top deck a wedding party fills the lobby, and the wedding planner is
missing one thing: the cake. Bumbusik carries it all the way from the kitchen aft, three tiers swaying at every step,
past bouncing basketballs and through Champs, and hands it over. The party moves into the chapel and the way forward
is clear. With the officer's card every gold door opens, down to the bridge.

**The bridge.** Bumbot sits on the ship's console. Behind him the security officer is frozen mid-creep with his net.
She crouches and calls him softly, and he starts to come to her hand; the officer swings, the cat springs, and the net
comes down on the alarm's big red button instead. Bells ring, the emergency
hatch in the ceiling drops open, and Bumbot shoots up its ladder and out.

**Sunset.** Through the hatch she comes out onto a small balcony over the bow, and the sun is going down; the whole
ship turns gold. Far below, at the very tip of the bow, a small black shape sits looking out to sea. She opens the
parasol and jumps, gliding down through the gusts, dodging two gulls, onto the mooring deck.

All day she has chased him, and all day he has run. Here, at the end of the ship, she stops chasing. She stands still
in the wind with her arms open, and this time Bumbot comes to her. She bends down, and he jumps into her arms.

**Home.** She carries him back through the ship she opened up during the day, and the people she helped come out to
clap her in. In cabin 8206 her husband is waiting. Bumbot curls up on the bed between them, and the day is done. The
next day he is still asleep there, and the ship is hers to wander.

## Step by step

### Region 1: the lower ship (decks 3, 4 and 5)

1. **The start.** The intro ends on deck 3's crew corridor: she steps out of her cabin. She goes up the service
   ladder to deck 4.
2. **The gangway officer** stands at the shell door on deck 4 and can't leave his post. His bubble shows a tray:
   he wants his lunch.
3. **The lunch** waits at the Seven Seas' pass (deck 5 aft), and **Bumbot is on the counter eating it.**
   **Near-catch 1:** at the sight of him she stops and screams «Кісюнічка!!!», and a moment later dashes at him. He
   bolts along the counter behind her and knocks the fruit bowl off its end. From then on D is **the dash** (a plate
   says so once: "D to dash").
4. She takes a fresh tray and carries it to the gangway: the first carry, with a tray's rules (a low hop, no
   ladders, a bump spills it). The officer eats and hands her the **Deck card**.
5. **The way on:** the midships crew trunk's blue reader (decks 4 and 5), up into Bliss on deck 6.

All the public stairs up from deck 5 are blocked: the security officer at his "SEARCH IN PROGRESS" rope, a
cleaner, a rehearsal rope, a photographer.

### Region 2: the atrium and the promenade (decks 6 and 7)

6. Deck 6 opens: the atrium, the shops, the promenade, the Library. The security officer's rope on the forward
   stairs lifts: those stairs are a shortcut now. In the atrium Bumbot rides the glass elevator up where she can't
   follow.
7. **Near-catch 2, in the Library:** Bumbot is asleep on top of the return trolley, by the librarian's desk. She
   creeps up on tiptoe and reaches for him; he leaps off, bolts aft and knocks the "QUIET PLEASE" rope stand over into
   the Library's aft door, which swings open and stays so. That is the way into region 3.

### Region 3: Showtime (the theatre, deck 7 aft and middle)

8. **Near-catch 3, on the stage:** Bumbot washes himself in the spotlight during the rehearsal. She creeps up; the
   security officer bursts out of the wings and leaps at him, his net catches the hanging lamp, and his weight on it
   sends Bumbot up on the fly rope out of reach; he drops through the stage's trap door.
9. **The stage manager** sneezes (he is allergic to cats): his bubble shows a pill bottle. His pills are in his
   cabin on deck 4 aft. She goes down the theatre's one-way trunk to deck 4, takes them, and comes back up the aft
   stairs (open now). He gives her the **Entertainment card**.
10. **The way on:** the crew trunk's red reader in Bliss (deck 6), up to deck 9's midships corridor. Deck 7 also
    opens forward through the Arcade, the casino and Magnum's, a shortcut back to the atrium.

### Region 4: Housekeeping (deck 9 midships)

11. She arrives in deck 9's corridor beside the linen room, whose door needs the green card.
12. She goes down the whole corridor, past its hazards, to **the steward**, whose trolley is jammed across its aft
    end. E at the trolley's end and walking: **pushing**. She frees it, and he tosses her the **Housekeeping
    card**.
13. Back to **the linen room** (green door; its door at the room's aft end, the room forward of it). Bumbot is
    asleep on a shelf. The laundry cart stands in her way with a stack of pillows in it, too tall to hop: she can
    only push it forward. **Near-catch 4** (remade 2026-11-24): as the cart bumps his shelf he leaps at the cover on
    the mouth of the ventilation shaft on the forward wall, knocks it to the floor and goes in; his spring scatters
    the pillows, and the cart is empty.
14. She pushes the cart on to the mouth and jumps onto it (the first time she stands on it, the hint "↓ to crawl"),
    and from its rim goes in on hands and knees (↓, then ←): **crawling**. **The shaft** slides her down a slope behind the wall and runs forward between decks 9 and 8, at
    its true length, to a grille over deck 8's forward corridor that gives way under her: she drops in past the
    corridor's shut lobby door. One way.

*This sequence was remade on 2026-11-23 (the linen room mirrored and its door one cabin door aft, the shaft at its
true place, deck 8 itself showing under its grilles): `docs/shaft-remake-log.md`.*

### Region 5: deck 8 forward

15. A calm stretch: cabin 8206 is here, and it is her checkpoint from now on. Its lobby door opens from inside. A
    glimpse: Bumbot's tail going up the forward stairs.
16. **The way on:** the forward stairs, up past decks 9 and 10 (their gold lobby doors stay shut; she stays in the
    stairwell) to deck 11.

### Region 6: the Lido (deck 11)

17. **Grip socks** on the gym's rack: **wall jumps**.
18. **Near-catch 5, at the pool:** Bumbot is fishing at the pool's edge, and he gets away from her. The kid with
    the water pistol squirts him into the pool; he scrambles out, leaps onto the drain's lever, which goes over
    under him, and bolts, and the pool drains. A hatch on its floor (down its ladder, out of a ceiling panel) drops
    into deck 10's midships hallway. That is not needed for the finale, but it
    is the only way into that optional hallway (delivery 2 is there); its lobby doors then stay open once she has
    walked out through them.
19. **The way on:** at the Great Outdoors, by her husband's table and the ice cream machine, a climb with the
    socks: off the service station's roof, over the parasol, a wall jump off deck 12's end wall onto the sun cover,
    and up it to deck 12's aft terrace. As region 8 opens, the blocks on the aft and midship stairs up to deck 12
    lift.

(The ice cream errands, for the kid and for her husband, are optional.)

### Region 7: outside (optional)

Not needed for the finale: the balconies, the boat walkway and the hallways are fast ways fore and aft.

### Region 8: the top deck and the funnel (deck 12)

20. **The engineer** at the technical door wants a coffee. It comes from the machines by the ice cream on deck 11:
    a hot tray that cools. She carries it up the aft stairs and through Teppanyaki, past the chef's flare.
21. He drinks, waves her up and climbs the ladder. She follows: the roof, the stairway up the funnel's front
    (gusts, gulls), its top. Bumbot sits up there on the funnel's lattice.
22. **The horn:** E at the cord. The blast sets off a cascade:
    - Bumbot shoots down the funnel and runs forward toward the bow;
    - the security officer jumps, **drops his card** on the sun deck and runs for the bridge;
    - the old lady bolts from her sunbed and leaves **her parasol** behind;
    - the gulls lift off the bow.
23. She picks up the **Officer's card** and **the parasol** (it opens only while jump is held after a press made
    in the air, and lets her glide).

### Region 9: the bow

24. **The wedding** fills deck 12's forward lobby, and the stairs forward are blocked. The wedding planner's
    bubble shows a cake.
25. The cake is in Cagney's kitchen (deck 12 aft). She carries it forward past the court's basketballs, the sun
    deck and Champs. It sways with every start, stop, turn and landing, and goes over if it sways too far. The
    party goes into the chapel, and the lobby clears.
26. The **Officer's card** opens the gold doors: down the forward stairs to deck 10's officers' corridor and the
    bridge's door.
27. **Near-catch 6, on the bridge:** Bumbot sits on the console. She calls him softly and he comes toward her hand; the
    security officer, creeping up behind him with his net, swings; the cat springs, and **the net hits the alarm's button**. The emergency hatch
    in the ceiling opens, and Bumbot shoots up its ladder.

### Region 10: the sunset and the finale

28. Up the hatch to the balcony forward of the spa. The sun is setting, and the camera shows Bumbot settling at the
    tip of the bow.
29. **The glide:** on the parasol, about four decks down to the mooring deck, through gusts and past two gulls that
    cross her path. A miss puts her back on the balcony.
30. **The catch:** at the tip of the bow, running at him drives him back. Standing still, he comes to her: arms
    wide, she bends, and he jumps into her arms.
31. **The way home:** with him in her arms (nothing can scare her now), through the mooring deck's door into
    deck 7 and up through the ship she opened, to cabin 8206. Four of the people she helped clap her in, and her
    husband is waiting. Bumbot curls up on the bed between them. **The win screen.**
32. **The day after:** a key, a fade, and the game goes on at midday, Bumbot asleep on the bed, so anything left
    to find can still be found.

## The chain in short

Lunch → **Deck card** → Bliss → Library (Bumbot opens the aft door) → theatre → pills → **Entertainment card** →
deck 9 → steward's trolley (**push**, **Housekeeping card**) → linen room (Bumbot into the shaft; **crawl**) →
deck 8 forward → forward stairs → deck 11: **grip socks** → the climb to deck 12 → engineer's coffee → the horn
(**Officer's card**, **parasol**) → the wedding cake → the bridge (the alarm, the hatch) → the sunset glide → the
catch → home to cabin 8206.
