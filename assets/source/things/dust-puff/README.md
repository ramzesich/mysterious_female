# Dust puff (the Library's bookcase sliding open on the reading nook)

Made 2026-11-22 with PixelLab's effects workbench (free): the `smoke_puff` preset scaled to 0.4, its colours a dusty
beige ramp (#6e5a48, #9a8670, #c2b29a, #e2d6c2), no outline. Saved to the user's My effects as "Bookcase dust puff",
effect id 7504c427-54e1-4561-a398-b2adf316768c (step 03a46d49-62f4-4d00-bb64-dd5c2945f226).

`bookcase-dust-puff.png`: 20 x 21 cells, 4 to a row, 10 frames (8 drawn, 9 and 10 empty); copied as
`assets/sprites/dust-puff.png` and played by `oddBookPull()` in `src/world/bow.js`, frames 0 to 7.
